Design directions

Four ways to rebuild the Triptych site, each with the real hero, the three looks and the download section. Every page follows your system's light or dark setting; add ?theme=light or ?theme=dark to force one.

  • A · GalleryThe logo hung as the artwork with a wall label; the three looks as framed panels; a catalogue to take it home. Instrument Sans.
  • B · Dot matrixNothing OS as a page: the logo lit dot by dot, Doto everywhere, round widgets, one red light. Doto and Space Mono.
  • C · EditorialA music magazine: a serif nameplate, a lead story with a red drop cap, a three-column spread, an index. Fraunces.
  • D · Pinned posterThe name stays pinned while the three looks scroll beneath it; the whole Download band is one button. Cabinet Grotesk.
