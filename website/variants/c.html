Triptych

A music player for the music you already own.

Triptych plays the files on your own Jellyfin server, on Windows, Linux, Android and iPhone. No subscription, no ads, no account of ours. It looks like the streaming apps you know, in three different ways, and you choose which.

Download Triptych

One library, three ways to look at it

Spotify

The familiar dark look: rows of cards, a green accent and big bold type. If you are leaving a streaming app, start here and nothing will feel new.

Apple Music

Frosted glass over your cover art, bare rounded artwork and slim pill sliders. The artwork shrinks when you pause, the way it does on an iPhone.

Nothing

Black and white with one red light, dot-matrix headings and lyrics, and thin outline icons. The red follows whatever accent colour you pick.

Get Triptych

Latest release v2.3. Install it, open it, and sign in with your Jellyfin server address.

  1. WindowsInstaller, .exe
  2. AndroidAPK
  3. LinuxAppImage
  4. Linuxsnap
  5. iPhoneUnsigned .ipa

Windows updates itself from inside the app. On Android, allow installs from your browser once; on iPhone, sideload with AltStore or Sideloadly.

Triptych is free and open source, made by one developer. Not affiliated with Jellyfin, Spotify, Apple or Nothing.
