# Cookie & Local Storage Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-18_

JellyWave does not use cookies, and does not use any advertising or analytics trackers. The app does use ordinary local storage mechanisms that exist to make it function at all, not to track you across sessions or devices:

- Local storage / Capacitor Preferences — app settings and cached UI state, kept only on your device.
- An encrypted local file — your Jellyfin session, so you don't have to log in every launch.

None of this is shared with any third party, and none of it is used to track you across other apps or sites. You can clear it at any time by logging out (clears the session file) or by uninstalling the app (clears everything).

See also the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) and [Data Deletion](DATA_DELETION.md).
